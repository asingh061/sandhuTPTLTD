Routes connecting Zambia

Operating key freight corridors across Southern Africa — from the Copperbelt to the Atlantic and Indian Ocean ports.

Atlantic coast port — a critical export hub for landlocked Southern Africa. Long-haul westbound corridor.

About Sandhu Transport

Sandhu Transport is a freight and logistics company that plans, implements and operates supply chain solutions on a national, regional and global scale. With a fleet of 90+ trucks and a team of over 130 people, we keep goods moving and commitments delivered — every time.
